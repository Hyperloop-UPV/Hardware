# BOM risk register

> Generated by `bom/build.py`. Do not edit by hand.

Two axes, deliberately not collapsed into one number:

- **Risk** is the likelihood of a supply or availability problem, scored from the part's flags. The arithmetic is in `risk_model` in the BOM model.
- **Impact** is what happens if the line fails in service.

A low-risk, safety-impact line is a real category here: the connector is easy to buy today and its failure drops the pod. It belongs at the top of the attention list even though its risk score is zero.
